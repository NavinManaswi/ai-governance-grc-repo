## 🏗️ GRC Ontology & Structure

The portfolio follows a **4-Layer Pyramid** GRC structure, ensuring serialized dependencies:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ LAYER 4b: IMPROVE (Respond) │
│ Regulatory Change & Audit Defense │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 4a: OPERATE (Monitor) │
│ Continuous Monitoring & Incident Response │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 3: CONTROLS (Assess & Mitigate) │
│ AI Risk Assessments (AIRA) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 2: INVENTORY (Discover) │
│ Use Case Classification (UCCF) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 1: FOUNDATION (Govern) │
│ Governance Framework & Policies │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘


> **Golden Rule:** Each layer depends on the layer below it. You cannot monitor what you haven't fixed, and you cannot fix what you haven't discovered.

## 🏆 Key Artifacts

### 1. [AI Risk Assessment — CreditIQ System](03-risk-assessments/creditiq-aira.md)

A comprehensive, 10-section risk assessment for a high-risk credit underwriting AI system, covering:

- System overview and data governance
- Inherent risk categorization (EU AI Act Tier 1)
- Detailed risk identification (7 risks, including algorithmic bias)
- Quantitative measurement (Disparate Impact Ratio, SHAP explainability)
- Risk treatment and mitigation plan
- Residual risk assessment with sign-off
- Regulatory mapping (EU AI Act, US laws, ISO 42001)

**Key Finding:** The model produced a **74% Disparate Impact Ratio** for racial groups — below the 80% threshold. Mitigations were implemented at a cost of $75,000, preventing a potential **$440 million** settlement exposure.

---

### 2. [Use Case Classification Framework (UCCF)](02-use-case-classification/uccf-framework.md)

A Triple-Gate classification methodology that:

- **Gate 1:** Filters prohibited practices (EU AI Act Art. 5)
- **Gate 2:** Triggers high-risk classification (Annex III)
- **Gate 3:** Differentiates limited vs. minimal risk

The UCCF is the "front door" to the entire governance program, ensuring risk-proportionate controls.

---

### 3. [Implementation Guidance Policy](04-implementation-guidance/implementation-policy.md)

Operationalizes the governance framework into enforceable controls across the AI lifecycle:

| Phase | Controls |
|-------|----------|
| **Design** | UCCF classification, system context, data governance |
| **Build** | Secure development, AI-BOM, reproducibility |
| **Test & Validate** | Performance, bias, security testing; AIRA completion |
| **Deploy & Monitor** | Human oversight, continuous monitoring, incident response |

---

### 4. [Incident Response Runbook](05-monitoring-incident-response/incident-response-runbook.md)

Severity-tiered incident response protocol:

| Severity | Reporting Timeline | Example |
|----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Critical** | 2-10 days to authority | Fundamental rights violation |
| **High** | 15 days to authority | Material financial harm, significant bias |
| **Medium** | Internal within 24 hours | Operational disruption |
| **Low** | Internal within 7 days | Minor errors, user complaints |
