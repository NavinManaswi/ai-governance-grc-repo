## 🎯 About This Portfolio

This repository contains a **comprehensive, audit-ready AI Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) portfolio** for a hypothetical global financial services firm, **NovaTech Financial Group**.

The portfolio demonstrates:

- ✅ **Strategic Governance Design** — Three-tier operating model, Three Lines of Defense, AI CoE
- ✅ **Regulatory Expertise** — EU AI Act, US state laws, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF
- ✅ **Operational Execution** — AI Risk Assessments, Implementation Guidance, Monitoring & Incident Response
- ✅ **Thought Leadership** — Published articles on enforcement trends and shadow AI risks
