# 📊 AI Risk Assessments (AIRA)

## Overview

This folder contains comprehensive **AI Risk Assessments (AIRA)** for NovaTech Financial Group's high-risk AI systems. Each assessment follows the NIST AI RMF framework and covers:

1. System context and data governance
2. Inherent risk categorization
3. Detailed risk identification (7+ categories)
4. Quantitative measurement (bias, performance, security)
5. Risk treatment and mitigation plans
6. Residual risk assessment with sign-off
7. Regulatory mapping (EU AI Act, US laws, ISO 42001)
