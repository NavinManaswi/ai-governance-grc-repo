# NovaTech Financial Group
## AI Governance Council — Charter

**Document ID:** GOV-CHR-2026-001

**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026

**Owner:** Chief AI Officer (CAIO)

**Approval:** Board AI Oversight Committee (15 February 2026)

---

## 1. Purpose

This charter establishes the AI Governance Council as the primary decision-making body for AI governance at NovaTech Financial Group. The Council ensures that AI systems are developed, deployed, and monitored in alignment with regulatory requirements, risk appetite, and ethical principles.

---

## 2. Governance Operating Model

### 2.1 Three-Tier Structure

| Tier | Body | Composition | Responsibilities |
|------|------|-------------|------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Board AI Oversight Committee | 2 independent directors + Chief Risk Officer | Annual AI strategy approval, risk appetite setting, material incident oversight |
| **Tier 2** | AI Governance Council (Monthly) | CAIO (Chair), CISO, Chief Privacy Officer, General Counsel, Chief Data Officer, Head of Model Risk | AI policy approval, risk classification decisions, major incident response, budget allocation |
| **Tier 3** | AI Risk Working Group (Weekly) | AI architects, data scientists, compliance officers, business unit AI leads | Operational risk management, control implementation, documentation, monitoring |

### 2.2 Three Lines of Defense

| Line | Role | AI-Specific Responsibilities |
|------|------|------------------------------|
| **1st Line** | Business units & AI developers | AI system design with embedded controls, documentation, self-assessments, incident identification |
| **2nd Line** | AI Governance & Risk teams | Policy setting, risk classification, independent reviews, training, oversight |
| **3rd Line** | Internal Audit | Independent assurance, control testing, regulatory compliance audits |

## 4. Council Responsibilities

### 4.1 Strategic Responsibilities

- Approve the annual AI strategy and budget
- Set and review AI risk appetite
- Approve major AI investments (>$1M)
- Oversee the AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE)

### 4.2 Risk Responsibilities

- Approve risk classification decisions for contested cases
- Review and approve AI risk assessments for Tier 1 systems
- Oversee the AI incident response process
- Review material AI incidents and root cause analysis

### 4.3 Compliance Responsibilities

- Approve AI policies and standards
- Monitor regulatory changes and assess impact
- Ensure compliance with EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and other applicable frameworks
- Approve external AI audits

### 4.4 Reporting Responsibilities

- Provide quarterly reports to Board AI Oversight Committee
- Maintain minutes of all Council meetings
- Publish an annual AI governance report

---

## 6. Decision-Making Process

### 6.1 Voting Procedures

- **Simple Majority:** Standard decisions
- **Supermajority (2/3):** Risk classification overrides, policy changes, budget approvals
- **Consensus:** Preferred for all decisions; vote only when consensus cannot be reached
- **Abstention:** Members with conflicts of interest must abstain

### 6.2 Escalation Paths

| Decision Type | Authority |
|---------------|-----------|
| Operational AI decisions | AI Risk Working Group (Tier 3) |
| Risk classification (Tier 1) | AI Governance Council (Tier 2) |
| Risk classification overrides | AI Governance Council (Tier 2) — 2/3 majority |
| Material incident response | AI Governance Council (Tier 2) |
| AI strategy, budget, policy | Board AI Oversight Committee (Tier 1) |
| Annual risk appetite | Board AI Oversight Committee (Tier 1) |
