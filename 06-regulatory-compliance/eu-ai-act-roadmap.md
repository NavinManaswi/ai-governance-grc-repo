# NovaTech Financial Group
## EU AI Act Compliance Roadmap

**Document ID:** COMP-EU-2026-001

**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026

**Owner:** AI Compliance Officer

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The EU AI Act represents the world's first comprehensive AI regulation. It imposes mandatory obligations on providers and deployers of AI systems based on risk tier. This roadmap outlines NovaTech's path to full compliance.

---

## 2. Key Deadlines

| Deadline | Obligation | Applicability |
|----------|------------|---------------|
